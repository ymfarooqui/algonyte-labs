## Problems & Pain Points
**Core problem:** Service businesses don't have a lead problem. They have a follow-up problem. Leads come in; the owner can't answer in the moment; by the time they call back, the prospect has already booked with the next shop on Google.

**Why alternatives fall short:**
- **$99/mo AI bots:** Single-channel (usually voice only). No qualifying logic, no follow-up, no calendar integration, no CRM.
- **Virtual assistants:** $1,500–$3,000/mo. They sleep, take vacation, quit. Not 24/7. Can't reply in under a minute.
- **Generic marketing agencies:** Run ads but don't fix the follow-up leak — most of the budget gets wasted on form-fills that never become bookings.
- **DIY stacks (Calendly + Zapier + GoHighLevel):** Pieces exist, but nobody is running them as a system. The site doesn't talk to the calendar, the calendar doesn't talk to payments, none of it ranks.
- **Doing it yourself:** Most owners lose 20–40% of leads to slow replies.

**What it costs them:**
- Direct revenue: missed jobs. One missed job per month covers the Starter plan. Two covers Growth.
- Wasted ad spend: $3,000 on Google ads → 40 form fills → 70% already booked elsewhere before the team calls back.
- Time: owner answers calls during dinner, on weekends, on the job — instead of doing the work.
- Reputation: unhappy customers leave reviews; happy ones don't — so Google ratings stagnate below 4.5.

**Emotional tension:** Stress of every ring of the phone. Fear of leaving money on the table. Doubt about whether "AI" is a real thing or just hype. Burn from prior vendors who promised and didn't deliver. Frustration with juggling 3+ vendors who blame each other when something breaks.
